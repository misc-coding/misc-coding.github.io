Realized forecast validation

Forecasts matched to ground truth.

Each point compares a published forecast with Open-Meteo observations at the same city and time. Rainfall is accumulated over the identical initialization-to-valid-time interval.

City
Variable

One initialization at a time

Forecast and ground truth over the horizon.

Method

A clean, comparable forecast slice.

Only common 00 UTC cycles with all required models and target leads are published. Missing data stops publication; a previous run is never silently substituted.

01

Align

All sources are cropped to the same India-region bounding box and exact lead endpoints.

02

Normalize

Temperature is converted to °C. Precipitation becomes millimetres accumulated since initialization.

03

Reduce
